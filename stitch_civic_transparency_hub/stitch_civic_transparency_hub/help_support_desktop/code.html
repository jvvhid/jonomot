Help & Support

Find answers to common questions about Jonomot or reach out to our team directly. We are here to ensure your voice is heard transparently and efficiently.

quiz Frequently Asked Questions

How is my data protected when I submit a report?

Jonomot employs end-to-end encryption for all user submissions. Your personal information is anonymized before reports are aggregated and displayed publicly on the platform, ensuring your identity remains secure while contributing to civic transparency.

What types of institutions can I review?

You can review a wide range of public and civic institutions, including municipal offices, public health clinics, educational facilities, and utility service providers. If an institution is missing, you can suggest it via the 'Add Institution' feature.

How long does it take for a report to appear on the public feed?

Reports typically appear within 24 hours after passing our automated moderation filters designed to prevent spam and ensure adherence to community guidelines.

Can I update or delete a submitted report?

Yes. You can manage your submissions from the 'Dashboard' section of your profile. You have the option to edit the details or permanently delete the report from the public record.
